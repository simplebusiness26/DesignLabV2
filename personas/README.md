# Tournament expert dossiers

These files are evidence-backed **method dossiers**, not impersonation claims. They are designed to make each contestant apply a distinct expert's documented frameworks, priorities, recurring critiques and body of work to the target app.

## Architecture
- Jesse James Garrett — layered product/experience structure
- Peter Morville — information architecture and findability
- Luke Wroblewski — mobile-first prioritization and capability
- Alan Cooper — goal-directed product structure

## UX
- Alan Cooper — goal-directed interaction

- Josh Clark — touch, direct manipulation and emerging interfaces
- Aarron Walter — emotional design and human product behavior

## UI
- Gleb Kuznetsov — high-craft product/visual systems
- Meng To — design-to-code native polish and motion
- Michael Flarup — identity, iconography and visual personality
- Sebastiaan de With — award-winning native app craft, tactile clarity and disciplined complexity

Each stage-specific dossier contains its evidence sources and an anti-caricature section where needed. The goal is not to copy a designer's signature appearance. It is to transfer documented decision principles into a new product context.

The fourth UI slot is now Sebastiaan de With. Halide Mark II, designed by Lux Optics with de With as co-founder/creative lead, won Apple's 2022 Design Award for Visuals and Graphics. His dossier is particularly relevant to complex native app surfaces such as camera experiences.
