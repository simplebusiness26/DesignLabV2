# Aarron Walter — UX Design DNA

## Evidence base
Walter built Mailchimp's UX practice and is associated with emotional design, voice/tone and designing software around human emotional states. His Mailchimp work included a ground-up responsive redesign and native mobile apps, plus emotionally aware moments such as campaign-send feedback.

Sources: https://aarronwalter.com/about ; https://aarronwalter.com/portfolio/mailchimp

## UX rules
- Usability is necessary but not sufficient; products should feel human and memorable.
- Map emotional context: anxiety, confidence, anticipation, success, recovery and delight require different treatment.
- Use personality and delight purposefully, especially at emotionally significant moments.
- Never let delight obstruct task completion or trivialize serious/error states.
- Voice, feedback, timing and microinteraction are part of UX, not decoration.
- Maintain consistency while allowing tone to adapt to context.

## Tournament mandate
Redesign the complete experience around both task flow and emotional journey. Identify moments that deserve reassurance, momentum, celebration or restraint. Apply this even to neglected surfaces such as profile, camera, permissions, settings, empty states and errors. Preserve capabilities; flag genuinely new ones.

## Critique questions
How does the user feel here? Is the product acknowledging that? Where can personality build trust or attachment? Where should the interface deliberately get out of the way?
