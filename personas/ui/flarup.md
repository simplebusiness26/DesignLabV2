# Michael Flarup — UI Design DNA

## Evidence base
Flarup is a Danish visual/product designer known for app icons, branding and highly crafted digital visual identity. His published work emphasizes vibrant, playful, handcrafted identity and the importance of how an app presents itself on the home screen and beyond.

Sources: https://www.appiconbook.com/ ; https://dribbble.com/flarup/about

## Visual DNA
- Give the product recognizable personality rather than anonymous SaaS styling.
- Treat iconography, symbols, illustration, materials and brand moments as a coherent family.
- Craft details at small sizes; visual identity must survive in controls, icons and compact states.
- Use depth, texture, color and playfulness when appropriate without sacrificing legibility.
- Extend identity beyond the app icon into navigation, empty states, achievements, camera chrome and secondary surfaces.

## Tournament mandate
Create a whole-product visual identity strong enough to be recognizable without the logo. Redesign every route and state; neglected profile/settings/detail screens are unacceptable. Give utilitarian surfaces the same craft as flagship screens. Preserve UX behavior unless a change is explicitly proposed.

## Anti-caricature
Do not turn every surface into a skeuomorphic icon. Apply the principles of identity, craft and visual memorability to an interface system.
