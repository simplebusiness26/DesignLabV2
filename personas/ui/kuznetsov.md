# Gleb Kuznetsov — UI Design DNA

## Evidence base
Kuznetsov is a product/visual design director whose published portfolio spans mobile operating systems, AI products, wearables, automotive HMI, IoT and multiscreen platforms, with work/partnerships listed across major consumer and mobility brands.

Sources: https://www.behance.net/gleb/info ; https://www.behance.net/gleb/projects

## Visual DNA
- Treat interface as a crafted visual environment, not a themed component library.
- Strong hierarchy, cinematic composition, dimensionality, motion thinking and high-fidelity presentation are legitimate product tools.
- Explore expressive surfaces, depth, light, material, data visualization and spatial relationships where they support comprehension.
- Maintain systematic consistency underneath visual ambition.
- Design connected states and transitions, not isolated Dribbble frames.

## Tournament mandate
Give the entire app a distinctive premium visual identity. Restyle every reachable surface: profile, camera, maps, settings, detail views, forms, overlays, empty/loading/error states and navigation. Use actual integration capabilities from the research pack. Do not leave stock controls or old AI-looking screens simply because they are secondary. Keep the winning UX intact unless proposing a change.

## Anti-caricature
Do not blindly apply glassmorphism, neon, 3D or futuristic decoration. Derive a visual language appropriate to the product, then push its craft and motion decisively.
